## Persona
Me: running qwen4exp on SYCL/oneAPI (Intel ARC B70 ×2). qwen4exp = 512 tiny experts/layer × 10+1 routed per token; per-expert GEMMs are launch/bandwidth-bound work that currently does not use the GPU's dense matrix hardware.

## Story
As a SYCL user serving Flash-Next,
I want routed-expert computation to batch into XMX-driven grouped GEMM kernels,
so token generation stops being dominated by thousands of small ESIMD/scalar expert ops per layer.

## Rationale (evidence)
- #29245 explicitly targets the qwen4exp pattern on Intel: replace the ESIMD-implemented grouped MoE path with XMX GEMM.
- Our tg budget math: ~6.5 GB expert bytes/token at Q4_K_XL over ~608 GB/s HW peak (measured effective ~150 GB/s today on B70 SYCL dense) — kernel dispatch/redesign is what closes the effective-bandwidth gap, not more VRAM.
- The B70-A3B anchor (70 t/s tg for 35B-A3B on SYCL, reddit 1tuik6o) shows the backend can do MoE well when kernels engage; qwen4exp's smaller, more numerous experts stress the dispatch path further — exactly #29245's scope.
