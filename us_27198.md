## Persona
Me (rig): second B70 rides PCIe4/PCH (likely x4), so tensor-split activation traffic is the wrong shape anyway — layer split is my daily mode. This story is **mostly for upstream health** (other dual-Intel users: #27198's rig is my exact model pairing) and optional future flexibility.

## Story
As a llama.cpp+SYCL user on multi-Intel-dGPU boxes,
I want ggml VMM pools peer-mapped across the device contexts they hand out (or the buffers that cross devices allocated where both contexts can reach),
so `--split-mode tensor` and `dev2dev_memcpy` work instead of taking the whole driver down with DEVICE_LOST.

## Rationale (evidence)
- #27198 contains verified working workarounds (`GGML_SYCL_ENABLE_VMM=0`, `GGML_SYCL_DEV2DEV_MEMCPY=2`) proving the diagnosis is correct and narrow — this is a plumbing gap, not hardware.
- Maintainer comment thread shows appetite: intel/compute-runtime#995/#996 accepted as driver deps; #29459 in progress.
- Without it: `-sm tensor` stays disabled for qwen4exp (#27941/#28569 chain), and every dual-Intel marketplace stays on env-var voodoo.
