## Story
As a llama.cpp+SYCL user on multi-Intel-dGPU boxes,
I want ggml VMM pools peer-mapped across the device contexts they hand out (or the buffers that cross devices allocated where both contexts can reach),
so `--split-mode tensor` and `dev2dev_memcpy` work instead of taking the whole driver down with DEVICE_LOST.
